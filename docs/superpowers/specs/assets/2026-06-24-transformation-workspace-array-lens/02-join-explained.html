What Iris's join does / doesn't do today
  • Inner join only — frame rows whose cell isn't in the right table are dropped.
  • Right must be unique on the key — one row per cell. Many-to-many (a key matching several right rows, which would multiply your rows) is deliberately refused for now.
  • The right side can be its own little pipeline — it may be reduced & collapsed to its grain first (e.g. a per-cell median computed over its own frames), then merged. That's how two features measured at different grains meet on a shared axis.
  • Only new columns are added — the join key and any column you already have aren't duplicated.
