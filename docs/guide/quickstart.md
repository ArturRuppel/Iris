# Quickstart

The quickest way to learn Iris is to build one analysis. This short, interactive
tutorial walks you through the whole shape of the tool — import a table, make a
plot, read the test Iris suggests — using a real dataset, right in the app. It
takes about five minutes, and it drives the actual interface: everything you do
here is the real thing, not a demo.

You will use two of the app's three views: **Data**, where your table lives, and
the **Workbench**, where the analysis is built. The tutorial opens each at the
right moment and points you at the next step.

[Start the tutorial](iris-tutorial:quickstart)

When you finish, you will be left with the analysis you built — a two-group
comparison of petal length in two iris species — ready to explore, edit, or save
as a `.iris` file. From there, the rest of this guide covers shaping tables,
working with more than one table, and refining figures.

Prefer to poke at a finished analysis instead? [Open one in Iris](iris-open:iris-species-comparison)
and explore the same figure and numbers hands-on.
